Our Story

Family recipes, neighborhood table.

Baobab Kitchen started in 2019 as a single charcoal grill outside Mama Fatuma's house in Oysterbay. Neighbors kept asking for "just one more skewer" until the grill became a kitchen, and the kitchen became a home for anyone craving real nyama choma.

Every recipe on our menu has been passed down, tasted, and approved by the family before it ever reaches your plate. We still buy from the same fish market and the same butcher we did on day one.
