Our Story

Built on the shoreline, not just beside it.

Serengeti Crest was raised from local coral stone and dhow timber, shaped by the same craftsmen who have worked this coastline for generations. Every suite faces the water. Every evening ends the same way — gin and tonic in hand, watching the sky do something it has never done quite the same way twice.
